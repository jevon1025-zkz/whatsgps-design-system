# Role Persona Notes

Use these distinctions only to adapt PC hierarchy, permissions, search, copy, and operational density. They do not define APP features or APP information architecture.

## Ordinary End User

- Usually manages personal or a small number of devices.
- Primary questions: location, current state, recent track, alarms, expiry, and service availability.
- Needs direct object identity, plain status language, and clear recovery/contact-service-provider guidance.
- Avoid organization management, customer-tree, inventory, or bulk operations without permission.

## Government/Enterprise Or Organization Manager

- Manages a larger device set, members, departments, roles, or operational reporting.
- Needs scanning, comparison, filtering, bulk handling, traceability, and permission-aware customer/organization context.
- Search and navigation should reduce route depth while preserving scope boundaries and auditability.
- Reminder copy may direct this role to complete an operation rather than contact a service provider, according to the approved source copy.

## Dealer Or Service Provider

- Manages customer relationships, device lifecycle, renewals/upgrades, service resources, and support across accounts.
- Needs customer/device cross-search, batch operations, clear ownership, resource availability, and role-safe destination links.
- Reminder copy commonly expects direct renewal/handling; ordinary-user wording commonly asks the user to contact the service provider.

## Shared Rules

- Role changes information priority and permitted actions, not the truth of device state.
- Do not show counts, suggestions, recent items, or match reasons outside the user's permission scope.
- Keep the same approved alarm/reminder content across PC and mobile for the same role and event.
- Do not derive a mobile navigation model from these persona notes.
